# Component instructions

These instructions apply to shared UI under `components/`.

- Keep health information calm, direct, and easy to scan. Do not turn neutral
  observations into warnings, obligations, or coaching claims.
- Components present decisions made by shared domain logic; they do not rederive
  freshness, cadence, clinical judgment, unit conversion, or authorization.
- Preserve accessibility, keyboard behavior, focus management, and reduced-motion
  behavior.
- Prefer established components and interaction patterns over local variants.
- A loading or pending treatment must not erase useful current content.

- Read the relevant documents in `docs/internals/`, especially `copy.md`,
  `design-doctrine.md`, `component-tests.md`, `overlays.md`, `micro-motion.md`,
  and `stateful-affordances.md`.
